# What happens next

From signing to answering your phone. Roughly two to three weeks,
because we go live carefully rather than quickly.

---

## The stages

### Stage 1 — You tell us about your business

*Your time: about 30 minutes*

You fill in the [setup form](02-setup-form.md), or we go through it on a
call. Services, prices, hours, area, and what counts as an emergency.

### Stage 2 — We build it

*Your time: none*

We configure your receptionist and connect everything up. You don't need
to do anything.

### Stage 3 — You connect your calendar

*Your time: about 5 minutes*

We send you a dashboard login. You click one button to connect Google
Calendar. We then make a test booking and you check it appeared
correctly.

### Stage 4 — We test it, hard

*Your time: none*

We run it through every scenario we can think of — booking, awkward
questions, price pressure, emergencies, someone trying to talk it into
something. We record the result of every one.

### Stage 5 — You listen to it

*Your time: about 30 minutes*

**This is the important one.** You ring the number yourself and try to
catch it out. Ask for a price it shouldn't know. Ask for a Sunday. Tell
it something's leaking gas.

You sign off the greeting and the pricing wording. Nothing goes live
until you've heard it and you're happy.

### Stage 6 — Soft launch

*Your time: 10 minutes a day for a few days*

Your number starts forwarding to it **only when you don't answer**. You
carry on exactly as you do now; it picks up what you'd have missed.

We review every single call for the first three days and tell you what
we find.

### Stage 7 — Full service

Once you're happy, it takes everything you don't answer. We keep
watching, and you get a summary each month.

## What we need from you, and when

| Stage | We need |
|---|---|
| 1 | The setup form filled in honestly |
| 3 | Five minutes to connect your calendar |
| 5 | Half an hour to test it and approve the wording |
| 6 | A quick look at the calls each day for the first few days |
| Throughout | Someone who answers when it transfers an emergency |

The two that genuinely block everything: **the calendar connection** and
**someone answering emergency transfers**. Everything else we can work
around.

---

## Why the phased start

We could point your number at it on day one. We don't, for one reason:
if something needs adjusting, we'd rather it affected a call you were
going to miss than one you'd have answered yourself.

Forward-on-no-answer means the worst case in the early days is a caller
getting the same outcome they'd have got anyway. That's a small risk.
Full forwarding on day one is not.
