## The five-minute morning check

1. **Messages** — anything marked *needs review*
2. **Bookings** — today and tomorrow look right
3. **Calls** — anything marked *failed* or *transferred*

That's it most days.

---

## Overview

Your numbers for the last 30 days: calls answered, how many were outside
your hours, appointments booked, messages taken, and how many it handled
without needing you.

If a figure shows "—", it means we haven't measured it yet, not zero.
We'd rather show you nothing than a number we can't stand behind.

---

## Calls

Every call, newest first. Each row shows when, the last four digits of
the caller's number, how long it lasted, and what came of it.

Click any call for the full picture:

- **Summary** — what happened, in two or three lines
- **Transcript** — everything said, both sides
- **Recording** — if you have recording switched on
- **Booking or message** — whatever came out of it
- **Timeline** — the order things happened in

You can search by the caller's last four digits or a customer name, and
filter by outcome, urgency, or date. **Export** gives you a spreadsheet.

### What the outcomes mean

| | |
|---|---|
| **Booked** | Appointment made and in your calendar |
| **Message taken** | Details captured for you to follow up |
| **Transferred** | Put through to a human |
| **Answered inquiry** | Question answered, nothing needed |
| **Caller hung up** | They rang off |
| **Failed** | Something went wrong — worth a look |

## Bookings

Everything booked, with the customer, service, time, and address.

**Cancelling:** you can cancel from here. Two things to know — it comes
off your calendar, and **it does not tell the customer**. Ring them
yourself.

Cancelled bookings stay in your history rather than disappearing, so you
can always see what was arranged.

---

## Messages

Everything the receptionist took for you.

- **Mark reviewed** once you've dealt with it, so the list stays useful
- **Add a note** for your own team

> Notes are private to your team. The receptionist cannot see them and
> will never read one to a caller.

Filter by urgency, or by what still needs reviewing.

## Usage

*Owners only.* Minutes used this month and calls answered, so there are
no surprises on your bill.

## Things worth knowing

**Calls appear within about a minute** of ending — the summary is
written after the call finishes.

**A "failed" call is worth reading.** Usually a caller rang off early,
but occasionally it's something we should fix. Tell us.

**If the calendar disconnects, booking stops.** It takes messages
instead and we'll get an alert. You can reconnect it yourself from
Integrations.

**Nobody else can see your data.** Each business is separated at two
independent levels. Another business on the platform cannot see your
calls even by accident.
