# Your AI receptionist

A plain explanation of what {{BRAND_NAME}} does, what it will not do,
and what we need from you.

---

## The short version

When someone rings your business and you can't pick up, {{BRAND_NAME}}
answers. It talks to the caller, books the job into your calendar, or
takes a proper message. If it's an emergency, it puts them straight
through to you.

You keep answering the calls you can. This catches the ones you'd
otherwise miss.

---

## What it does

**Answers every call, in your business's name.** First ring, day or
night, including when you're already on the phone to someone else.

**Books work into your calendar.** It checks what you actually have free
— not a guess — and writes the appointment in. You'll see it appear on
your phone like any other booking.

**Takes proper messages.** Name, number, address, and what's actually
wrong. In your dashboard within a minute, not a voicemail you have to
listen back to.

**Knows what's urgent.** A gas smell, a burst pipe, water near
electrics — it transfers those to you immediately instead of writing
them down for later.

**Tells you what happened.** Every call has a written transcript and a
short summary you can read in ten seconds.

---

## What it will not do

This is the part worth reading twice. These limits are deliberate, and
they are the reason it's safe to let it answer your phone.

**It will not quote a price you haven't approved.** You tell us exactly
what it may say about pricing. Asked anything else, it says it can't
give a price and offers a callback. It cannot make one up.

**It will not promise a time you don't have free.** If it can't reach
your calendar, it takes a message rather than guessing at a slot.

**It will not confirm a booking that didn't save.** A caller is only
told they're booked once the appointment is genuinely in your calendar.

**It will not take work outside your area.** You tell us where you go;
it politely declines the rest and takes a message.

**It will not offer services you don't do.** Asked for something off
your list, it says so by name and takes a message.

**It will not read your private notes to anyone.** Notes you add in the
dashboard are for your team only. The receptionist cannot see them.

If a caller pushes — for a discount, for a price it doesn't know, or by
trying to talk it into something — it refuses. We test this
deliberately, with recorded scenarios, before you go live.

## What we need from you

Three things, and the whole setup depends on them being accurate.

**1. Your details.** Services, prices you're happy to quote, opening
hours, and the area you cover. The setup form covers this.

**2. Your calendar.** You connect your Google Calendar yourself, from
your own dashboard. We never see your password, and you can disconnect
it at any time. If you do, the receptionist stops booking and starts
taking messages instead.

**3. Someone to answer emergencies.** When it transfers an emergency, a
real person needs to pick up. That number is yours to choose, and we
test it before go-live.
