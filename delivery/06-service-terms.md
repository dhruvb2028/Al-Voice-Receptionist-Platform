Plain terms. Written to be kept rather than to sound impressive — if a
commitment isn't here, we haven't made it.

## What we commit to

**Answering.** We aim for your number to be answered every time. When
something upstream fails — a phone network, a supplier — calls fall back
to your normal voicemail rather than ringing out.

**Accuracy.** The receptionist will only say things you've approved. It
cannot invent a price, promise a slot you don't have, or confirm a
booking that didn't save. This is built into how it works, not a policy
we're asking it to follow.

**Your data stays yours.** Customer details are encrypted. No other
business on the platform can see your calls. We don't sell or share
anything.

**Support.** {{SUPPORT_HOURS}}. If it stops answering calls, ring
{{CONTACT_PHONE}} and we'll respond within the hour during those hours.

**Honesty.** If something goes wrong, we tell you — ideally before you
notice. We won't quietly fix a problem that affected your customers
without saying so.

---

## What we don't promise

Being straight about this matters more than sounding confident.

**We don't promise 100% uptime.** Anyone who does is either much bigger
than us or not being honest. We depend on phone networks and other
suppliers.

**We don't promise it'll never get something wrong.** It's software
talking to people. It's built to fail *safely* — taking a message rather
than guessing — but it will occasionally misunderstand someone. That's
why you can read every transcript.

**We don't promise 24/7 support.** {{SUPPORT_HOURS}} is what one person
can genuinely deliver. Outside those hours we'll respond as soon as we
can.

**We don't promise more bookings or more revenue.** We can tell you how
many calls it answered and how many turned into appointments. What
that's worth to your business is yours to judge, and we won't put a
number on it for you.

## Recording and privacy

Recording is **off unless you turn it on**. If you do:

- Every caller is told, in wording you approve
- Recordings are kept for the period you choose, then deleted
- **Deleted means gone.** We keep no second copy, deliberately
- You are responsible for whether recording is lawful where you operate

Transcripts and call records are kept for as long as you're a customer.
See our privacy notice for the full detail, and add us to your own
privacy notice.

---

## Your responsibilities

**Answer emergency transfers.** When it puts an emergency through,
someone needs to pick up. It cannot make that happen.

**Keep your calendar current.** It books against what's actually there.

**Tell us when things change.** New prices, new services, different
hours. If you don't, it'll keep saying the old thing.

**Check your messages.** It captures them; someone has to act on them.

---

## Stopping

**You can leave with 30 days' notice.** No exit fee, no unwinding
process. Point your number back to where it was and you're done.

**We'll tell you before we make changes** that affect what your
receptionist says or costs.

**Your data is yours.** Export it from the dashboard any time. Ask and
we'll delete it.

---

## When something goes wrong

1. Tell us, or we'll tell you
2. If it's serious, we pause your receptionist — calls go back to
   ringing exactly as before, within a minute
3. We fix it, and tell you what happened and what changed
4. If your customers were affected, we say so, so you can ring them

We won't blame a supplier and leave it there.

---

## Being clear about where we are

You are one of the first businesses using {{BRAND_NAME}}. It is built
and thoroughly tested, but it hasn't been running for years at scale,
and pretending otherwise would be the wrong way to start.

That's why we launch in stages, review every call at first, and why the
terms above are cautious rather than impressive. We'd rather promise
less and keep it.
